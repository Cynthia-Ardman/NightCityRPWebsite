**NCRP GUN SHOP BUSINESS PROGRESSION POLICY**
*Draft for Review - June 2026*

**Scope**
This policy applies to player-owned and player-operated gun shops. Ripperdoc, cyberware, Tech Shop, weaponsmith, explosives, and custom-gear rules will be handled separately.

**Core Rules**
- Every new gun shop begins at **Tier 0** unless an approved transition assigns an existing shop another starting tier.
- A higher tier makes a gun shop better at being a gun shop. It does **not** grant another business type.
- Business tier represents the shop's facilities, equipment, active staff, suppliers, reputation, and inventory capacity.
- Location, tier, staffing, and supplier access are separate requirements.
- Restricted and Special weapons are never normal gun-store inventory.
- On-map and off-map businesses use the **same employee requirements**.

Message 2/12

**PLAYER BUSINESS PARTICIPATION LIMITS**

These limits apply per player across all of their characters.

**Ownership Slot**
- One character may own, co-own, manage, or otherwise control **one business**.
- The owner character may operate their own business.
- The owner character may not be registered as an employee of another business.
- A controlling ownership share, profit share, management role, or "silent partner" arrangement still uses the ownership slot.

**Employment Slot**
- One separate character may be a full-time employee of **one other business**.
- That employee character may not own, co-own, manage, or control a business.
- That employee character may not work full-time for multiple businesses.

**Additional Characters**
- A third or later character may not own, manage, run, or be registered as an employee of a business.
- Additional characters may still visit, purchase goods, receive services, or appear in one-off scenes.
- Staffing is counted by **unique players**, not characters.
- A player's owner character and employee character cannot both count toward the same business.
- Alternate characters may not be used to transfer business inventory, funds, supplier access, or permissions.

Message 3/12

**LOCATION, RENT, AND STAFFING**

**Monthly Rent**
- **Tier 0:** Off-map 1,000 eb | On-map unavailable
- **Tier 1:** Off-map 2,000 eb | On-map 3,000 eb
- **Tier 2:** Off-map 3,000 eb | On-map 4,000 eb
- **Tier 3:** Off-map 4,000 eb | On-map 5,000 eb

Tier 0 is an off-map startup operation. Tier 1-3 may operate on-map or off-map.

The on-map premium represents the benefit of a persistent, visible VRChat location. Property purchase, construction, relocation, guards, cameras, reinforced doors, turrets, and other security upgrades are separate costs unless specifically included.

**Required Staffing**
- **Tier 0:** Proprietor only; 0 employees; 1 unique player total
- **Tier 1:** Proprietor + at least 1 full-time employee; 2 unique players total
- **Tier 2:** Proprietor + at least 2 full-time employees; 3 unique players total
- **Tier 3:** Proprietor + at least 3 full-time employees; 4 unique players total

These staffing requirements are identical for on-map and off-map businesses. The proprietor does not count as an employee.

Message 4/12

**TIER 0 - STARTUP GUNSMITH**

A new informal operation working from a garage, rented room, home workshop, mobile bench, or another off-map location.

**Staffing**
- Proprietor only
- No full-time employee required

**May Provide**
- Firearm cleaning and maintenance
- Routine repairs and basic troubleshooting
- Firearm appraisal and condition checks

**May Not Provide**
- Firearm sales
- Wholesale firearm orders
- Controlled, Restricted, or Special weapons
- Custom weapon construction
- Complex mechanical modifications

---

**TIER 1 - LOCAL GUN STORE**

A small established business with a storefront or off-map location, retail inventory, basic equipment, and ordinary legal or street-market suppliers.

**Staffing**
- Proprietor + at least 1 full-time employee

**May Provide**
- All Tier 0 services
- Basic firearms
- Basic ammunition
- Ordinary firearm accessories
- Standard replacement parts

**May Not Provide**
- Controlled firearms or ammunition
- Bespoke mechanical modifications
- Restricted or Special weapons

Message 5/12

**TIER 2 - ESTABLISHED ARMS DEALER**

An established commercial operation with improved equipment, reliable inventory, active staff, and a documented fixer, gang, specialist, or corporate supplier.

**Staffing**
- Proprietor + at least 2 full-time employees

**May Provide**
- All Tier 1 goods and services
- Basic and Controlled firearms
- Basic and Controlled ammunition
- Standard attachments
- Cosmetic customization
- Special orders for permitted Basic or Controlled weapons

**May Not Provide**
- Regular Restricted or Special weapon sales
- Unique or event-only weapons
- Unapproved bespoke mechanical weapon builds

---

**TIER 3 - MAJOR ARMS SPECIALIST**

A significant, well-equipped gun business with experienced staff, advanced facilities, and major faction, corporate, arms-dealer, or high-level fixer connections.

**Staffing**
- Proprietor + at least 3 full-time employees

**May Provide**
- All Tier 2 goods and services
- Complex special orders within permitted categories
- Approved custom weapon commissions
- Major corporate or faction contracts
- Brokerage or transfer services for individually approved weapons

Message 6/12

**TIER 3 LIMITS**

A Tier 3 gun shop may not:
- Treat Restricted or Special weapons as normal shelf inventory
- Claim unlimited access to military, iconic, unique, or experimental weapons
- Operate as a Tech Shop, Ripperdoc clinic, explosives manufacturer, drone workshop, or unrelated business

**CUSTOM WEAPONS**

"Custom" describes work performed on a weapon. It is not a weapon availability tier.

- Tier 2 permits standard attachments and cosmetic customization.
- Tier 3 permits approved custom commissions.
- Complex mechanical sidegrades, internal redesign, or fabrication may require a Weaponsmith Tech Shop under future rules.
- A gun shop may commission or broker another business's specialized work without gaining that business's permissions.
- Custom work cannot be used to disguise a Restricted or Special weapon as a lower-tier item.
- The base weapon and all components remain subject to their normal availability rules.

Message 7/12

**RESTRICTED AND SPECIAL WEAPONS**

Restricted and Special weapons are never normal gun-shop inventory, regardless of business tier.

They may only be acquired through:
- Fixer jobs or individually approved rewards
- Case-by-case black-market transactions
- Event-runner rewards
- Staff-approved storylines
- Specific megacorp or faction contracts

A Tier 3 shop may act as a broker, transfer location, legal front, or meeting point for an approved transaction. This does not create permanent supplier access.

**Example**
A Tier 3 shop may facilitate the transfer of one fixer-approved military rifle. The shop may not add it to its public catalogue or assume it can order ten more afterward.

A supplier relationship never creates an unrestricted catalogue and may be lost through inactivity, broken agreements, unpaid obligations, faction conflict, or story consequences.

Message 8/12

**UPGRADE COSTS AND WAITING PERIODS**

Upgrade costs represent a business front, equipment, inventory capacity, licensing, supplier deposits, renovations, and expansion. They are separate from monthly rent.

- **Tier 0 -> Tier 1:** 20,000 eb
  - Requires 2 complete billing cycles at Tier 0
- **Tier 1 -> Tier 2:** 40,000 eb
  - Requires 2 complete billing cycles at Tier 1
- **Tier 2 -> Tier 3:** 60,000 eb
  - Requires 2 complete billing cycles at Tier 2

**Rules**
- Waiting periods do not stack.
- Waiting periods cannot be skipped by paying extra.
- The shop must meet the requested tier's employee requirement when the upgrade is approved.
- Upgrade fees are the same for on-map and off-map shops.
- On-map rent already includes the location premium.
- Property purchase or construction is handled separately.
- The earliest normal path from Tier 0 to Tier 3 is 6 complete billing cycles and 120,000 eb in upgrade fees, before rent and other expenses.

Message 9/12

**UPGRADE ELIGIBILITY**

Completing the waiting period does not automatically grant an upgrade. The shop must:

1. Be current on rent and business obligations.
2. Complete the required two billing cycles at its current tier.
3. Meet the requested tier's proprietor and employee requirement.
4. Demonstrate meaningful business activity during the waiting period.
5. Remain registered exclusively as a gun shop.
6. Have an appropriate supplier, sponsor, or faction relationship.
7. Pay the full upgrade cost.
8. Receive fixer or business-team approval when required.
9. Receive explicit review for every Tier 3 upgrade.

**Expected Supplier Progression**
- **Tier 0:** Personal tools and retail parts
- **Tier 1:** Public market or ordinary wholesaler
- **Tier 2:** Fixer, gang, specialist, or minor corporate distributor
- **Tier 3:** Major arms distributor, megacorp sponsor, faction supplier, black-market contact, or established fixer network

Tier 3 supplier access supports advanced orders and approved brokerage. It does not grant unlimited Restricted access.

Message 10/12

**FULL-TIME EMPLOYEE DEFINITION**

"Full-time" is a rules designation, not a literal real-world hourly requirement.

A full-time employee must:
- Be formally registered to the business for the current billing cycle
- Be controlled by a unique player
- Use that character's single employment slot for the business
- Meaningfully participate through sales, repairs, inventory work, business scenes, appointments, or another approved activity
- Not exist only as a placeholder name used to satisfy a tier requirement

**Recommended Activity Baseline**
At least one meaningful business scene, transaction, service, or operational activity per employee during each monthly billing cycle.

This baseline may be adjusted after the system has been tested.

Message 11/12

**STAFFING LOSS AND DOWNGRADES**

- If a shop falls below its employee requirement, it enters a one-billing-cycle grace period.
- During the grace period, it keeps its current tier for existing operations but may not request another upgrade.
- Existing approved orders may be completed.
- New high-tier special orders may be paused or require fixer approval while the shop is understaffed.
- If staffing is not restored by the next billing date, the shop downgrades by one tier.
- Continued understaffing may cause further monthly downgrades.
- A shop may voluntarily downgrade at the start of a billing cycle to reduce rent and obligations.
- Upgrade fees are not refunded after a downgrade.
- Re-upgrading normally requires the standard waiting period, staffing, approval, and upgrade fee unless a specific exception is approved.

**CORE INTERPRETATION RULE**

Higher tier means deeper specialization, not broader permissions.

A Tier 3 gun shop is still only a gun shop.

Message 9/14

**MODIFICATIONS AND SIDEGRADES**

Techie modifications should normally be **sidegrades**, not permanent objective upgrades.

A valid sidegrade must provide a meaningful benefit and a meaningful tradeoff.

Possible tradeoffs include:
- Reduced reliability
- Higher power consumption
- Limited duration
- Longer cooldown
- Increased weight or visibility
- Reduced capacity, range, or damage
- Higher maintenance costs
- Increased heat or legal attention
- Risk of malfunction or bodily strain

**Rules**
- Tier 2 may perform approved standardized sidegrades.
- Tier 3 may propose bespoke custom sidegrades.
- Pure increases with no meaningful drawback require separate approval.
- Multiple modifications may not be stacked to erase each other's tradeoffs.
- Unless specifically approved, an item may only have one substantial Techie sidegrade at a time.
- All mechanical changes must be documented on the character sheet.
- Repairs restore normal function; they do not improve baseline performance.

Message 10/14

**WEAPONSMITHING SPECIALTY**

A Weaponsmith Tech Shop repairs and modifies weapons. It is not a retail gun store.

**Tier Progression**
- **Tier 0:** Diagnose, maintain, and repair customer-owned Basic firearms
- **Tier 1:** Basic parts, ordinary attachments, replacement components, and cosmetic work
- **Tier 2:** Controlled components and approved standardized weapon sidegrades
- **Tier 3:** Approved bespoke weapon commissions and case-by-case Restricted weapon work

**Limits**
- May not operate a general firearm storefront
- May not maintain normal firearm inventory for retail sale
- May not wholesale ammunition
- May not use custom work to bypass weapon availability
- May not manufacture explosives under this policy

Gun Stores sell firearms. Weaponsmiths perform advanced technical work on them. The two businesses may collaborate without becoming the same business.

Message 11/14

**CYBERWARE HARDWARE SPECIALTY**

A Cyberware Hardware Tech Shop sells, repairs, modifies, and fabricates cyberware hardware. It does not perform surgery.

**Tier Progression**
- **Tier 0:** Diagnose and repair removed cyberware hardware
- **Tier 1:** Standard cyberware hardware worth up to 2 Cyberware Points
- **Tier 2:** Standard hardware worth up to 3 Cyberware Points and approved sidegrades
- **Tier 3:** Hardware worth up to 5 Cyberware Points and approved bespoke custom work

**Rules**
- A Ripperdoc is required to remove or install cyberware.
- The Ripperdoc clinic must be capable of installing the Cyberware Point value involved.
- A Techie modifying cyberware does not gain permission to perform surgery.
- A Ripperdoc installing modified cyberware does not gain permission to fabricate it.
- Restricted, experimental, military, corporate-proprietary, or Special cyberware remains individually controlled.

Message 12/14

**OTHER TECH SHOP SPECIALTIES**

**Armor and Personal Gear**
- Repairs, sells, fabricates, and sidegrades armor or carried equipment

- Tier 3 unlocks approved bespoke work and case-by-case Restricted equipment

**Drones and Robotics**

- Controlled systems begin at Tier 2
- Advanced custom robotics begin at Tier 3
- Combat drones and autonomous lethal systems require separate approval

**Electronics and Security Systems**
- Covers cameras, sensors, communications, alarms, access systems, and surveillance hardware
- Tier 3 may create advanced integrated security systems
- Turrets and lethal defenses require separate approval and purchase

**Vehicle Technology**
- Covers vehicle repair, parts, tuning, and approved modifications
- Does not allow complete vehicle sales
- Vehicle weaponization requires separate approval

Message 13/14

**RESTRICTED AND SPECIAL TECHNOLOGY**

Restricted and Special technology is never normal Tech Shop inventory, regardless of tier.

It may only be obtained or worked on through:
- Fixer jobs
- Case-by-case black-market transactions
- Event rewards
- Megacorporate or faction storylines
- Direct staff-approved storylines

A Tier 3 shop may be approved to repair, modify, fabricate, or transfer one specific Restricted item.

That approval does not:
- Grant permanent access to the item
- Permit public advertising of it
- Allow additional copies to be created
- Permit reverse engineering for mass production
- Allow the shop to sell it to unrelated customers

A customer possessing Restricted technology does not automatically authorize the shop to modify or reproduce it.

Message 14/14

**UPGRADES, EMPLOYEES, AND DOWNGRADES**

**Upgrade Costs**
- **Tier 0 -> Tier 1:** 20,000 eb and 2 complete billing cycles at Tier 0
- **Tier 1 -> Tier 2:** 40,000 eb and 2 complete billing cycles at Tier 1
- **Tier 2 -> Tier 3:** 60,000 eb and 2 complete billing cycles at Tier 2

Waiting periods cannot be stacked, skipped, or reduced by paying extra.

**Upgrade Requirements**
- Rent and obligations are current
- Requested staffing requirement is met
- The shop demonstrated meaningful activity
- An appropriate supplier exists
- The shop remains within its registered specialty
- The full upgrade cost is paid
- Tier 3 receives explicit review

**Full-Time Employees**
A full-time employee must be registered to the business, controlled by a unique player, and meaningfully participate during the billing cycle. Placeholder employees do not count.

**Staffing Loss**
A shop below its staffing requirement receives a one-billing-cycle grace period. If staffing is not restored, it downgrades by one tier. Upgrade fees are not refunded.
